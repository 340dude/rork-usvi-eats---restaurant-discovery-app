## Full description (App Store & Google Play, ~4000 characters max)

**VI Eats is the easiest way to find great food across the U.S. Virgin Islands.**

Whether you're a local looking for your next go-to spot or a visitor exploring St. Thomas, St. John, St. Croix, or Water Island, VI Eats helps you discover restaurants that match what you're craving.

**Browse & Search**
- Explore restaurants by island, cuisine, price, and dietary needs
- Filter for waterfront seating, live music, kid-friendly spots, WiFi, and more
- Search by restaurant name, cuisine, or dish
- See which restaurants are open right now

**Restaurant Details**
- Full menus with prices, descriptions, and dietary tags
- Photos, hours, and contact info
- One-tap call, directions, and website links
- Distance from your current location

**Favorites**
- Save the restaurants you love and pull them up anytime

**List or Map View**
- Browse as a scrollable list or see everything on a map

**Help Keep Info Accurate**
- Spot outdated hours or a wrong menu price? Report it in a couple of taps

VI Eats covers the whole territory — no more guessing which restaurants are actually open, or scrolling through outdated reviews. Just real, current information about where to eat across the U.S. Virgin Islands.
