01

Information we collect

VI Eats is built so that browsing restaurants doesn't require an account. Here's exactly what we collect, and from whom.

DataFromRequired?
Device location Anyone browsing restaurants No — you can deny it
Favorited restaurants Anyone using Favorites Stored on-device only

Report description & optional photo Anyone submitting a correction No personal info required

We do not require you to create an account, provide your name, or give us your email just to browse, search, or favorite restaurants.

02

How we use it

  • To show restaurants near you and sort them by distance.
  • To let restaurant owners sign in and manage their own profile, menu, hours, and photos.
  • To give restaurant owners basic, aggregated engagement numbers for their own listing (e.g. how many people viewed their page this week).
  • To review and act on accuracy reports submitted about a restaurant.
03

Location data

When you allow location access, VI Eats reads your device's current position to calculate how far each restaurant is and sort your results accordingly. That's the only thing it's used for.

What we don't do
  • We never track your location in the background — only while the app is open and you're actively browsing.
  • We never store your location or send it to our servers. It stays on your device.
  • We never use your location for advertising.

You can deny location access, or turn it off later in your device settings, and still use every other part of the app — you'll just see restaurants unsorted by distance.

04

Sharing & third parties

We don't sell your information, and we don't run ads or use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs anywhere in VI Eats.

We do rely on a small number of service providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles restaurant-owner sign-in. Data submitted through the app (favorites are the exception — see below) passes through Supabase's infrastructure to reach us.
  • Unsplash — some restaurant photos are sourced from Unsplash's stock photo library. This doesn't involve any of your personal data.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of VI Eats, our users, or the public.

05

Retention & deletion

  • Favorites live only on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them completely — we never see them.
  • Restaurant owner accounts are kept while the account is active. Email us to request deletion.
  • Accuracy reports are kept as part of a restaurant's record so we can track and resolve issues over time.
06

Children's privacy

VI Eats is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

07

Security

Data sent between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit. Restaurant owner accounts are protected by access rules that restrict each owner to editing only their own restaurant's information — no owner can see or change another restaurant's data or reports.

08

Your rights & choices

  • Deny or revoke location access anytime in your device's privacy settings.
  • Clear your favorites by clearing the app's local data.
  • Ask us what data we hold about your restaurant-owner account, correct it, or request deletion, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live, local law may give you additional rights over your information. Contact us and we'll do our best to help.

09

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use VI Eats after a change means you accept the updated policy.
